# Baseline event study: insider purchases and sales (2015-2020)

> **Exploratory, survivor-only.** Free yfinance prices only cover companies that still trade; delisted, acquired and bankrupt issuers are missing. Development period 2015-2020 (2012-2014 is warm-up); no 2021+ returns were used. Treat every number below as a description of this sample, not as evidence of a tradable edge.

Generated by `uv run insider-whales event-study`; the sample, benchmarks, controls and statistics are described below. Event tables are in `data/processed/event_study/` and the full CSVs are in `reports/event_study/` (both git-ignored).

## Sample

- Events: 157,197 open-market insider transactions (54,698 purchases, 102,499 sales) from 3,390 issuers.
- Conservative event set only (the plan's `conservative` filter), price series that passed the Form 4 price-consistency check, first executable session in 2015-01-01 to 2020-12-31.
- Entry at the open of the first executable session after the filing became available; exit at the close of the session `h` sessions later, using the split- and dividend-adjusted total-return index. Returns whose exit falls after 2020-12-31 are not computed, so later horizons use fewer events, mostly 2020 ones.
- Sales are reported as the return of the stock after the sale (a negative number would mean the insider sold before a decline), not as a short-selling result.

## Adjustments

- **Minus SPY**: market-adjusted return over the same entry-to-exit window.
- **Minus sector ETF**: SPDR sector ETF chosen from the issuer's current SIC code (coarse prefix mapping; null for unclassified issuers, and XLRE before 2015-10 and XLC before 2018-06 do not exist).
- **Minus size ETF**: IWM for micro and small caps, MDY for mid, SPY for large and mega caps and unknown size.
- **Minus matched controls**: the 3 nearest stocks of the same universe and sector, matched on the session before entry by trailing 60-day return, 60-day volatility, 252-day drawdown and 20-day dollar volume, with no insider disclosure in the previous 90 days and at least $100,000 average daily dollar volume. Controls use the same entry and exit sessions and are averaged per event.
- Confidence intervals are two-way cluster-robust (issuer and calendar month). Calendar-time tables average each entry month first and use Newey-West standard errors.

## Limitations

- Survivor-only prices: issuers that were delisted, acquired or went dark are missing and delisting returns are unavailable. This flatters long-horizon results.
- Insider purchases and sales cluster in the same issuers and months; confidence intervals account for that, but the effective sample is far smaller than the event count.
- Sector is from the current SIC code, not point in time.
- Controls are matched on dollar volume rather than market capitalisation and only exclude stocks with an insider disclosure in the prior 90 days.
- No transaction costs, spreads or capacity limits are applied here; that is the backtest's job.
