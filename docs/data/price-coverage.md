Reason definitions:

- `no_ticker`: the filing gave no usable symbol (placeholder or unparseable).
- `delisted_or_acquired_likely`: not on the SEC's current listings and stopped filing before 2022; the provider has no history.
- `ticker_changed_unmatched`: listed today under a different ticker whose series did not match this event.
- `listed_but_provider_has_none`: listed today, yet yfinance returned nothing for the symbol.
- `went_dark_but_still_filing`: not currently listed but filed in 2022 (OTC, foreign or deregistered).
- `series_starts_after_event`: the symbol's history starts after the event (a later company or listing reused it).
- `series_ends_before_event`: the symbol's history ended before the event.
- `price_mismatch`: the series disagrees with the prices in the insiders' own Form 4s (recycled ticker or bad split data).
- `no_bar_near_entry`: the series has a gap of more than 7 days at the entry session.
- `fetch_error`: the download failed for the symbol and will be retried.
- `not_fetched`: the symbol has not been downloaded yet.
